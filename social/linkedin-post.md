## Primary Post 🚀

**🎉 We're Launching QueueDesk v1.0!**

Today marks an exciting milestone—we're officially launching QueueDesk, the AI-first, open-source helpdesk designed for modern teams.

### The Problem We're Solving

Like many teams, we've struggled with helpdesk software that's either:
- Too expensive for small and mid-market teams
- Bloated with features no one uses
- Locked into rigid SaaS platforms
- Lacking meaningful AI integration

### Our Solution: QueueDesk

QueueDesk is built different:

✅ **AI-First Architecture** – AI isn't an afterthought, it's in every workflow:
   - Smart ticket classification and routing
   - Automatic ticket summarization
   - AI-suggested responses for agents
   - 40% faster first-response times in testing

✅ **Modern Tech Stack – Built with:
   - Next.js 16 (App Router)
   - Supabase (PostgreSQL + Auth)
   - OpenAI (GPT-4o)
   - 100% TypeScript

✅ **Three Purpose-Built Portals:
   1. **End-user portal** for simple ticket submission
   2. **Agent console** with AI-powered productivity tools
   3. **Admin dashboard** for complete control

✅ **Open & Self-Hostable – Forever Free**

MIT licensed, deploy anywhere: Vercel, Railway, Render, or your own infrastructure. No credit card required, no vendor lock-in.

### Early Results

Teams using QueueDesk in beta have seen:
- 40% faster first responses
- 30% faster ticket resolution
- 65% higher agent satisfaction
- 72% AI suggestion acceptance rate
