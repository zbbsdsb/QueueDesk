## Introduction

Welcome to QueueDesk, your AI-powered internal service desk! This guide will help you submit requests, track their progress, and communicate effectively with support teams.

## Getting Started

### Accessing QueueDesk

1. Navigate to your QueueDesk workspace URL (e.g., `https://yourcompany.queuedesk.com`)
2. Log in using your company credentials or email/password
3. After successful login, you'll be directed to your dashboard

### Dashboard Overview

Your dashboard provides a quick overview of:
- Active tickets (in progress)
- Recently resolved tickets
- Quick actions for submitting new requests

## Submitting Tickets

### Through the Web Portal

1. Click **New Request** from the sidebar or dashboard
2. Select the appropriate request type (e.g., IT Support, HR Request, Access Request)
3. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Subject**: Brief title for your request
   - **Description**: Detailed explanation of your request
   - **Priority**: Select urgency level (P1 = Critical, P2 = High, P3 = Medium, P4 = Low)
   - **Attachments**: Add relevant files (max 25MB per file)
4. Review your submission
5. Click **Submit Request**

You'll receive an email confirmation with your ticket number and a link to track progress.

### Through Email

You can also submit tickets by sending an email to the designated queue email addresses (e.g., `it-support@yourcompany.com`):

1. Compose an email to the appropriate queue address
2. Use a clear subject line
3. Provide detailed information in the email body
4. Attach any relevant files
5. Send the email

The system will automatically create a ticket and send you a confirmation.

### Through Public Link (No Login Required)

If provided by your organization, you can use a public form link to submit tickets without logging in:

1. Access the public form URL
2. Fill in your contact information
3. Complete the request form
4. Submit the form

You'll receive an email with a secure link to track your ticket.

## Tracking Ticket Progress

### Viewing All Tickets

1. Click **My Tickets** in the sidebar
2. You'll see a list of all your tickets with:
   - Ticket number (e.g., QD-1234)
   - Subject
   - Current status
   - Last updated time
   - Assigned agent (if any)

### Understanding Ticket Statuses

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| New | Ticket has been received but not yet processed |
| In Progress | Agent is actively working on your ticket |
| Waiting on You | Agent needs additional information from you |
| Waiting on Approval | Ticket is pending approval from a manager |
| On Hold | Ticket is delayed due to external dependencies |
| Resolved | Issue has been addressed and ticket is pending closure |
| Closed | Ticket has been fully resolved and closed |
| Cancelled | Ticket has been cancelled |

### Filtering and Searching

- Use the search bar to find tickets by keyword
- Filter by status, date range, or queue
- Sort tickets by creation date, last updated, or priority

## Viewing Ticket Details

1. From the ticket list, click on any ticket to view details
2. The ticket detail page shows:
   - Full ticket history and timeline
   - All communications with agents
   - SLA (Service Level Agreement) status and deadlines
   - AI-generated summary (if available)
   - Any attached files
   - Approval status (if applicable)

## Communicating with Agents

### Adding a Comment

1. Open the ticket detail page
2. In the comment section, type your message
3. Attach files if needed
4. Click **Send Message**

Your message will be visible to the assigned agent and will trigger a notification.

### What You Can't See

Internal notes between agents are not visible to requesters to facilitate candid team collaboration.

## Profile Settings

### Updating Your Profile

1. Click your profile icon in the top right
2. Select **Profile Settings**
3. Update your:
   - Display name
   - Contact information
   - Notification preferences
4. Click **Save Changes**

### Notification Preferences

Configure when and how you receive updates:
- Email notifications for ticket status changes
- Email notifications for new agent comments
- Digest frequency (instant, daily, or weekly)

## FAQ

### Q: How do I re-open a closed ticket?

A: Closed tickets cannot be re-opened. If you have a similar issue or need further assistance, please submit a new request and reference the old ticket number.

### Q: What if I forget my ticket number?

A: You can find all your tickets in the **My Tickets** section, or search by subject or keywords.

### Q: How long will it take for my ticket to be resolved?

A: Resolution time depends on the priority and complexity of your request. Each queue has SLA targets that are visible on your ticket detail page.

### Q: Can I update my ticket after submission?

A: Yes! You can add comments or attachments at any time to provide additional information.

### Q: Who can see my ticket?

A: Only the assigned team, agents, and approved approvers can access your ticket. Your privacy is protected by role-based access controls.

### Q: What file types can I attach?

A: Most common file types are supported (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, images, etc.). Maximum file size is 25MB.

### Q: How do I cancel a ticket?

A: Open the ticket and click **Cancel Ticket** (if available). Note that some tickets may require approval to cancel.

For additional assistance, contact your workspace administrator or refer to the organization's internal support documentation.
